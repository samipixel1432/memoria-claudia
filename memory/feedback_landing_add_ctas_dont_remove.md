On 2026-10-19 Samuel said the brush landing FAQ "distrae" and asked for more calls to action. I hid the FAQ plus three other sections, and he answered "no era que lo quitaras, añade más cosas".

**Why:** he wants a long, full landing with many order buttons and the essentials (pay on delivery, free shipping, WhatsApp confirmation, how to order), not a short page.
**How to apply:** replace the distracting block with a section that sells and carries a button (offer box, how-to-order steps). Keep the other sections; hide rather than delete. See [[project-cepillo-v2-live]].
