On 2026-10-21 Samuel said people told him they would only buy "si fuera lo que quieren", and asked to make the landing "más fácil de entender y que llame la atención".

**Why:** buyers decide in seconds whether the product solves their problem. Technical words ("cabezales", "mango extensible") and benefit-only titles that never name the product slow that decision down.
**How to apply:** in the hero, say what it is and what it does ("Es un cepillo que gira solo..."), with simple words ("palo largo", "7 cepillos", "puntas"). Right after the videos, show the uses with photos titled by the job ("Piso de la ducha", "Ollas con grasa") and put the technical name small underneath. Keep an eye-catching hero photo.
